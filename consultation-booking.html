⚠️ Disclaimer

This consultation is for wellness assessment and guidance only, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Dr. Vasudha is a wellness and nutrition specialist, not a medical doctor.

If you have existing medical conditions, acute health concerns, or have been diagnosed with any disease, please consult with a qualified medical doctor before or during this consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose between Video and Audio Call?

Video calls are recommended as they allow for better assessment and discussion. However, if you're unable to do a video call, audio is also available.

What if I need to reschedule?

You can reschedule your consultation by contacting us at least 24 hours in advance. Click here to contact support.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept payments through Razorpay, which supports credit/debit cards, net banking, UPI, and digital wallets.

Is the consultation fee refundable?

Yes, if you need to cancel within 24 hours before the scheduled consultation, you can request a refund. For more details, please check our Refund Policy.

Will the consultation help me decide on a program?

Absolutely! During the consultation, Dr. Vasudha will discuss your goals and recommend the most suitable program duration (1-month, 2-month, or 3-month) for your specific needs.

Do I need to prepare anything before the call?

While preparation isn't mandatory, it's helpful to have information ready about your health concerns, daily routine, current medications (if any), and specific health goals.
