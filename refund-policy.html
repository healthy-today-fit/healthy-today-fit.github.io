Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear and transparent terms for cancellations and refunds

Last Updated: September 2026

Policy Overview

At Healthy Today Fit, we are committed to providing the best wellness programs and services. This page outlines our refund and cancellation policy for consultations and programs.

Key Principle: Once a program has started, no refunds or cancellations are possible. However, users always have the option to select a shorter program duration if circumstances change.

30-Minute Consultation Policy

Refund Eligibility (Within 24 Hours)

You are eligible for a full refund if you cancel your consultation booking at least 24 hours BEFORE your scheduled consultation time.

  • Full refund will be processed to your original payment method
  • Refunds typically take 5-7 business days to appear in your account
  • To request a cancelation or refund, contact us at: WhatsApp: +91 7483644571 (over Chat)

No Refund (Within 24 Hours)

If you cancel your consultation less than 24 hours before the scheduled time, no refund will be issued.

  • This allows us to offer the slot to other clients
  • You may request to reschedule your consultation instead, via WhatsApp: +91 7483644571 (over Chat)

Rescheduling Options

If you need to reschedule your consultation:

  • You can reschedule to another available slot at no additional cost
  • Contact us via WhatsApp: +91 7483644571 to reschedule
  • Rescheduling requests should be made at least 12 hours in advance
  • Available rescheduling date must be within 30 days of original booking

No-Show Policy

If you don't show up for your scheduled consultation:

  • No refund will be issued
  • You may request to reschedule the consultation
  • A new consultation fee will not be charged if you reschedule within 7 days of no-show

Wellness Programs Refund & Cancellation Policy

🔴 NO REFUNDS AFTER PROGRAM STARTS

Once a wellness program (1-month, 2-month, 3-month, or custom) has been started, no refunds or cancellations are possible under any circumstances.

Before Program Starts - Full Refund (Within 7 Days)

If you cancel your program enrollment BEFORE the program has officially started:

  • You are eligible for a 100% refund if cancellation is requested within 7 days of payment
  • The program must not have been initiated (no consultations, guidance, or materials provided)
  • Refunds will be processed to your original payment method
  • Typical refund timeline: 5-7 business days
  • To request a refund: Email us with your order details

After Program Starts - NO REFUNDS

Once your program has officially started (after first consultation or guidance is provided), no refunds are possible, regardless of the reason for cancellation.

This includes cancellations due to:

  • Personal circumstances or life changes
  • Lack of time or commitment issues
  • Results not meeting expectations
  • Medical reasons or health concerns
  • Any other reason whatsoever

Program Switching Option

Instead of cancellation, you have the option to select a shorter program duration:

  • From 3-Month to 2-Month: Before completion of 2 months, you can opt out instead of completing the full 3 months. The remaining 1-month fee may be adjusted based on the terms at the time of enrollment.
  • From 3-Month to 1-Month: Before completion of 1 month, you can opt out of the remaining months. Adjustments will be made based on the fees paid and the actual program duration completed.
  • From 2-Month to 1-Month: Before completion of 1 month, you can opt out instead of completing the full 2 months.

Important: Program switching requires mutual agreement and must be requested in writing to healthytoday.dr.vasudha@gmail.com. Terms and conditions of switching will be discussed and finalized.

What Constitutes "Program Start"

A program is considered to have started when:

  • Your initial consultation with Dr. Vasudha has been scheduled or completed
  • Your personalized health assessment has been conducted
  • Your personalized nutrition plan has been created or shared
  • You have received any guidance, recommendations, or materials from Dr. Vasudha
  • Support (WhatsApp or calls) has been initiated

Special Circumstances

Medical Emergency or Serious Health Issue

If you experience a serious medical emergency or health issue during the program:

  • You should immediately seek appropriate medical care
  • Contact us to inform about the situation
  • We may offer the option to pause or postpone the program temporarily
  • Once your health situation stabilizes, you can resume the program
  • A refund is not provided, but program duration may be adjusted based on circumstances

Technical or Service Issues

If there are technical issues or service disruptions on our end:

  • We will work to resolve the issue immediately
  • If the issue significantly impacts your program, we may offer compensation or program extension
  • Refunds are not typically provided for technical issues, but compensation options will be discussed

Payment & Refund Processing

Payment Methods

We accept payments through standard gateway, which supports:

  • Credit cards
  • Debit cards
  • Net banking
  • UPI
  • Digital wallets

Refund Processing

When a refund is approved:

  • Refunds are processed to your original payment method
  • Processing typically takes 5-7 business days
  • In some cases, it may take up to 10 business days depending on your bank
  • You will receive a confirmation email with refund details
  • Your transaction reference number will be provided for tracking

Duplicate Charges

If you believe you have been charged twice:

  • Do not make another payment
  • Contact us immediately with transaction details
  • We will investigate and refund any duplicate charges

How to Request a Refund

Step 1: Check if you're eligible (refunds within 7 days for programs, or 24 hours for consultations)

Step 2: Send an email to: healthytoday.dr.vasudha@gmail.com

Step 3: Include the following information in your email:

  • Your full name
  • Your phone number
  • Program/Consultation name and date of enrollment
  • Transaction reference/Order ID
  • Reason for refund request

Step 4: We will review and respond to your refund request within 3-5 business days

Step 5: Once approved, refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund if I don't see results?

No, refunds are not provided based on results or outcomes. Once the program has started, no refunds are possible regardless of the results. Our focus is on providing personalized guidance; individual results depend on many factors including adherence, lifestyle, and individual body factors.

What if I change my mind after starting the program?

Once a program has started, no refunds are possible. However, you can opt to discontinue the program and select a shorter duration if this aligns with your situation. The fees will be adjusted accordingly.

Is there a trial period?

There is a 7-day cancellation window BEFORE the program officially starts. Once the program has started (after first consultation or guidance), this window closes and no refunds are available.

Can I pause my program instead of cancelling?

Program pausing is handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact us to discuss your situation, and we may be able to work out an arrangement.

What if I'm not satisfied with the consultation?

Consultations are not refundable if cancelled less than 24 hours before the scheduled time. However, you can reschedule to another available slot at no additional cost.

How long does it take to get my refund?

Once a refund is approved, it typically takes 5-7 business days to appear in your account. Some banks may take up to 10 business days.

What if I don't receive my refund after 7 days?

If you don't receive your refund within 7 business days, please contact us with your transaction reference number, and we will investigate the issue with payment gateway.
