How to File a Grievance

Step-by-Step Process:

Step 1: Gather Information

Have ready: your full name, phone number, order/program ID, date of issue, and detailed description of the problem.

Step 2: Send Your Grievance

Email: healthytoday.dr.vasudha@gmail.com

Subject Line: "GRIEVANCE: [Brief description of issue]"

Message: Include all relevant details about your complaint

Step 3: Provide Details

Include:

  • Your full name and contact information
  • Order/Program ID and date of enrollment/transaction
  • Detailed description of the issue or complaint
  • Date when the issue occurred
  • What resolution you're seeking
  • Any supporting documentation (screenshots, receipts, etc.)

Step 4: Acknowledgment

You will receive an acknowledgment email within 7 business days confirming we've received your grievance.

Step 5: Investigation & Resolution

We will investigate your grievance and contact you within 3-7 business days with our findings and proposed resolution.

Step 6: Resolution Implementation

Once a resolution is agreed upon, we will implement it and follow up with you to ensure you're satisfied.

Common Grievance Categories

Service Quality Issues

Examples: Dissatisfaction with guidance quality, missed appointments, unprofessional behavior, lack of support

Resolution Process: We'll review the service provided and discuss ways to improve your experience or provide alternative solutions.

Payment & Billing Issues

Examples: Unexpected charges, duplicate payments, payment not reflected, billing disputes

Resolution Process: We'll verify all charges and refund any errors. For billing disputes, we'll provide clear documentation of what was charged and why.

Technical Issues

Examples: Website not working, payment gateway errors, communication platform failures

Resolution Process: We'll work with our technical team to resolve the issue and may offer program extension or compensation if significant disruption occurred.

Refund & Cancellation Disputes

Examples: Disagreement about refund eligibility, delayed refunds, cancellation policy interpretation

Resolution Process: We'll review the refund policy with you, explain our position, and work toward a fair resolution.

Program-Related Concerns

Examples: Issues with personalized guidance, concerns about program effectiveness, health-related worries

Resolution Process: Dr. Vasudha, Ph.D, will review your concerns and discuss modifications to your program plan or provide additional guidance as needed.

Professional Conduct Issues

Examples: Disrespectful communication, confidentiality concerns, inappropriate behavior

Resolution Process: We take professional conduct seriously. We'll investigate thoroughly and take appropriate action including apologies, corrective measures, or refunds if warranted.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I'm not satisfied with the resolution?

If you're not satisfied with our initial response, you can request escalation. Contact us via email with "ESCALATION REQUEST" in the subject line, and we will have Dr. Vasudha personally review your case.

Will filing a complaint affect my program?

No. We take complaints seriously and value customer feedback. Filing a grievance will not negatively impact your ongoing program or services.

How long do I have to file a grievance?

You can file a grievance at any time. However, for faster resolution, we recommend filing complaints within 30 days of the issue occurring.

What happens if I file multiple grievances?

All grievances are reviewed individually on their merit. Multiple grievances may be consolidated if they relate to the same issue.

Is my complaint information confidential?

Yes, all complaint information is kept confidential and is only shared internally with those involved in resolving the issue.

Can I request compensation for a grievance?

Compensation (refund, program extension, services credit) is considered on a case-by-case basis depending on the nature and impact of the issue.
