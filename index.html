Welcome to Healthy Today Fit

Healthy Today Fit is a personalized health and lifestyle program designed to help you manage lifestyle-related health concerns through customized nutrition, exercise, lifestyle changes, and holistic wellness practices.

Our approach focuses on understanding your individual body, digestion, lifestyle, health condition, and goals rather than following a generic diet plan.

Why Choose Our Program?

Unlike one-size-fits-all diets, we create personalized plans based on your unique body type, gut type, health conditions, and lifestyle. With support from Dr. Vasudha Ph.D., you'll receive evidence-based guidance combining modern nutrition science with Ayurvedic and functional medicine principles.

Your Body Is Unique

Everyone's body is different, which is why the same diet or exercise routine may not work for everyone.

Before creating your personalized plan, we consider your body type and gut type and understand your lifestyle, food habits, daily routine, stress levels, health conditions, and personal goals.

Our approach incorporates concepts from Ayurveda and functional medicine, including body-type assessment based on Vata, Pitta, and Kapha principles.

Personalized Gut-Health Approach

Digestion and food tolerance can vary from person to person. Certain foods that are considered healthy may still cause discomfort or digestive problems for some individuals.

Our assessment looks at your digestive patterns and individual needs so that your nutrition plan can be customized accordingly.

Depending on your symptoms and assessment, areas such as IBS, bloating, digestive discomfort, and other gut-related concerns may be considered while designing your plan.
